# ITEM & SLOP TROUGH Dual-Economy Constitution

## Status

Draft economic canon derived from the monetization theory visual.

## Core distinction

```txt
ITEM captures value through meaning, durability, provenance, and scarcity.
SLOP TROUGH captures value through volume, contrast, discovery, and transformation.
```

The two systems may fund one another, but neither may erase the other.

## ITEM economy

ITEM revenue may come from:

- primary sales of physical or digital artifacts
- secondary-market royalties where lawful and technically enforceable
- commissions and institutional design work
- canon licenses
- provenance and verification services
- vault, archive, and research subscriptions
- exhibition, museum, and collector services
- manufacturing packets and certified editions
- patronage and sponsored preservation

Money may not purchase canon placement, approval, provenance strength, or board rank.

## SLOP TROUGH economy

SLOP TROUGH may monetize the infrastructure around abundance without optimizing for addiction.

Permitted revenue paths:

- optional creator tools
- paid compute or batch-analysis services
- sponsored challenges with visible labeling
- institutional trend and corpus analysis using aggregated, privacy-preserving data
- API access to public, licensed, or synthetic datasets
- curation and moderation services
- design-recovery bounties
- opt-in licensing of artifacts or extracted traits
- patronage of public archives and research

Forbidden defaults:

- sale of private personal behavior
- covert surveillance
- manipulative infinite-scroll optimization
- dark patterns
- undisclosed sponsored ranking
- pay-to-win judgment
- resale of private submissions
- commercial use of contributor work without explicit permission

## Golden Dust

Golden Dust is initially a non-transferable contribution and impact accounting unit, not a speculative token.

It records verified actions such as:

- original contribution
- useful critique
- successful refinement
- provenance verification
- trait discovery
- canon impact
- manufacturing contribution
- maintenance of public infrastructure

Golden Dust may later unlock access, reputation, governance weight, discounts, patron recognition, or revenue participation only through explicit rules and legal review.

It must not be marketed as an investment, currency, security, or guaranteed store of value.

## S-Dust

S-Dust is initially a non-transferable activity and discovery credit, not a financial asset.

It may measure:

- useful browsing and classification
- duplicate detection
- slop-pattern tagging
- failed-experiment recovery
- moderation assistance
- contribution to corpus cleanup

Passive attention alone should not mint meaningful value. The system should reward useful interpretation, classification, and recovery rather than raw time spent.

## Value-recovery loop

```txt
synthetic abundance
→ Slop Field exposure
→ public and machine classification
→ recoverable trait detection
→ contributor consent
→ ITEM refinement
→ design lineage
→ prototype or licensed use
→ contributor attribution and compensation
```

SLOP TROUGH produces contrast, discovery, and raw material.

ITEM produces preservation, trust, and durable value.

## Contributor revenue participation

When a contribution or extracted trait enters commercial use, the system must preserve:

- source artifact
- contributor identity or chosen pseudonym
- applicable license version
- transformation permissions
- commercial-use consent
- attribution preference
- compensation formula
- dispute and takedown path
- downstream lineage

Possible compensation models include:

- fixed acquisition fee
- milestone bounty
- pooled royalty share
- design-family participation
- edition-specific royalty
- institutional commission split

No model is automatic until implemented contractually and reviewed for legal compliance.

## Strategic principles

1. Monetize what lasts on ITEM.
2. Monetize useful transformation on SLOP TROUGH, not addiction.
3. Popularity is not canon.
4. Attention is a signal, not ownership.
5. Data exhaust is not automatically a product.
6. Money cannot buy judgment.
7. Commercial value must preserve contributor lineage.
8. Golden Dust and S-Dust begin as internal accounting, not speculative assets.
9. The system should prefer revenue from tools, services, artifacts, and institutions over behavioral advertising.
10. Every monetization mechanism must be auditable and reversible.

## Product implication

The visual theory is directionally correct as a two-pole economy, but the production system should implement:

```txt
SLOP TROUGH
volume → classification → recovery → consent → transformation

ITEM
judgment → preservation → provenance → scarcity → durable value
```

The strongest business is not "ads versus collectibles."

It is a contribution refinery that turns abundant synthetic material into traceable, consented, durable artifacts and design intelligence.
