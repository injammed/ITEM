# Slop Field, ITEM Boards, and the 10,000 Immutablia

## Status

ITEM canon and product north star.

This document defines the relationship between the public Slop Trough field, the ITEM preservation boards, the canon gate, and the eventual first 10,000 immutablia.

## Core System

```txt
SLOP FIELD
→ worst synthetic excess rises through anti-quality signals

ITEM BOARDS
→ strongest artifacts rise across many distinct categories

CANON GATE
→ provenance, critique, refinement, and trait extraction

10,000 IMMUTABLIA
→ finite physical-digital artifact corpus assembled from the strongest traceable contributions
```

The Slop Trough is not the final product.

The boards are not merely entertainment.

The contribution system exists to gather, classify, judge, refine, and preserve enough diverse human-machine artifact intelligence to design a finite corpus beyond the capacity of one creator.

## Slop Field

The Slop Field intentionally makes the worst safe synthetic material visible.

It should rank artifacts by anti-quality dimensions such as:

- derivative repetition
- contextlessness
- provenance weakness
- aesthetic collapse
- empty spectacle
- incoherence
- engagement bait
- accidental grotesqueness
- failed experiment value
- synthetic excess

The leading Slop boards represent the strongest examples of failure, excess, repetition, or collapse.

They are useful because they:

- establish contrast
- expose failure patterns
- produce negative training examples for human judgment
- identify fragments that may still contain one valuable trait
- keep low-signal abundance from polluting the preservation boards

Slop ranking must not become harassment of contributors. Rank artifacts and patterns, not personal worth.

## ITEM Boards

ITEM boards rank the strongest artifacts across a wide variety of classes.

There must not be one universal taste leaderboard.

Each artifact can enter multiple boards when justified by its extracted traits.

## Judgment Model

Public judgment begins with:

```txt
PRESERVE
REFINE
SLOP
```

But board ranking must also use structured dimensions:

- novelty
- coherence
- provenance strength
- technical feasibility
- symbolic durability
- visual distinction
- manufacturability
- contribution to an existing design lineage
- contribution of a new reusable trait

Public votes are signals, not sole canon authority.

Curator and expert review remain necessary for safety, provenance, feasibility, and final preservation.

## Artifact Genome

The system must extract reusable traits from artifacts rather than treating every upload as indivisible.

```ts
export type ArtifactTrait = {
  id: string;
  artifactId: string;
  category:
    | "geometry"
    | "material"
    | "optics"
    | "mechanism"
    | "iconography"
    | "denomination"
    | "security"
    | "provenance"
    | "narrative"
    | "interface"
    | "manufacturing"
    | "utility"
    | "ritual"
    | "other";
  label: string;
  description: string;
  sourceContributionId: string;
  contributorId: string;
  provenanceStrength: number;
  noveltyScore?: number;
  feasibilityScore?: number;
  curatorStatus: "proposed" | "verified" | "rejected";
};
```

A weak complete artifact can contain one strong trait.

A strong artifact can contribute many traits.

Every extracted trait must preserve its source and contributor lineage.

## Canon Gate

The canon gate routes submissions through:

```txt
submission
→ metadata and provenance
→ quarantine
→ safety and rights review
→ public/curator judgment
→ critique
→ refinement
→ trait extraction
→ board placement
→ AETIMM candidate / refine / reject / SLATRA
```

Nothing reaches the 10,000 immutablia corpus through popularity alone.

## The 10,000 Immutablia

The first 10,000 immutablia are a finite designed corpus of distinct physical-digital artifacts.

They are not 10,000 randomly selected uploads.

They are assembled from a traceable contribution graph:

```txt
many contributions
→ verified traits
→ design lineages
→ synthesis candidates
→ expert feasibility review
→ prototypes
→ final immutable issuance records
```

Each immutablis should have:

- unique identifier
- artifact family
- complete design lineage
- credited contributors
- material specification
- geometry specification
- optical/mechanical specification when applicable
- symbolic function
- manufacturing packet
- provenance record
- edition/issuance status
- physical verification record

## Contributor Covenant

The platform must not silently convert user contributions into commercial object designs.

Before contribution traits enter commercial prototype work, the system must record:

- submission license
- attribution preference
- transformation permission
- commercial-use consent
- compensation or royalty terms when applicable
- takedown and dispute path
