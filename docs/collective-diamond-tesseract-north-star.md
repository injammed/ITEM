# Collective Diamond Tesseract North Star

## Status

ITEM canon and product-direction document.

This is the deeper motive beneath the SLOP TROUGH intake surface.

## Core Objective

AETIMM is not merely a receptacle for synthetic media.

It is intended to become a mass-participation artifact-design system capable of collecting, comparing, refining, preserving, and recombining contributions from thousands or millions of people toward the design of extraordinary physical-digital objects.

The long-horizon object class is:

```txt
AETIMM diamond tesseracts
```

These may include:

- private personal or institutional artifacts;
- collectible continuity objects;
- synthetic-diamond or fused-quartz objects;
- complex optical, mechanical, and digital provenance systems;
- denomination-inspired art objects;
- clearly labeled USD-referential experimental objects;
- future government-authorized currency objects only if formally commissioned and legally approved.

## Hidden Motive, Stated Clearly

The public-facing SLOP TROUGH solves an immediate problem:

```txt
AI-made excess needs somewhere to go.
```

The deeper system solves a harder problem:

```txt
How can a civilization-scale population collectively design artifacts that exceed the imagination and technical reach of any single creator?
```

The trough is therefore an intake gravity well.

It captures:

- visual forms;
- materials ideas;
- mechanical concepts;
- denomination systems;
- security patterns;
- iconography;
- provenance methods;
- manufacturing techniques;
- interface concepts;
- symbolic systems;
- failures worth learning from.

## Collective Design Loop

```txt
millions of synthetic and human-directed contributions
→ provenance capture
→ quarantine
→ public and expert judgment
→ preserve / refine / contain
→ motif extraction
→ design-family clustering
→ technical feasibility review
→ prototype synthesis
→ physical artifact candidates
→ tested editions
```

The desired outcome is not a popularity contest.

The desired outcome is a continuously improving design corpus from which exceptional physical artifacts can be engineered.

## Artifact Genome

Every accepted contribution should eventually be decomposable into reusable design traits.

```ts
export type ArtifactGenome = {
  visualMotifs: string[];
  geometry: string[];
  materials: string[];
  opticalEffects: string[];
  mechanicalIdeas: string[];
  securityIdeas: string[];
  denominationIdeas: string[];
  iconography: string[];
  manufacturingMethods: string[];
  symbolicFunctions: string[];
  provenanceStrength: number;
  feasibilityScore?: number;
  noveltyScore?: number;
};
```

A contribution may fail as a whole while still containing one valuable trait.

## Tesseract Design Families

The system should eventually maintain evolving families such as:

### Private Continuity Tesseracts

Personal, family, institutional, or collector objects centered on identity, memory, provenance, and inheritance.

### Public Denomination Artifacts

Non-circulating denomination-inspired objects that explore scarcity, trust, national iconography, optical security, material permanence, and public meaning.

### USD-Referential Experimental Objects

Art and design studies that reference United States currency aesthetics or denominations while remaining explicitly non-legal-tender and non-governmental unless formally authorized.

### Sovereign Commission Candidates

Design packages suitable for later presentation to governments or authorized monetary institutions.

These cannot be represented as official currency, partnership, approval, or legal tender without written authorization.

## Bitcoin-Mogging Standard

"Bitcoin-mogging" is treated as an internal design challenge, not a financial claim.

The target is to exceed familiar digital scarcity objects in dimensions they do not primarily occupy:

- physical presence;
- material difficulty;
- optical uniqueness;
- mechanical complexity;
- cultural density;
- provenance richness;
- durability;
- ritual and display value;
- inspectability without network access;
- collective authorship.

Do not claim guaranteed monetary superiority, investment return, or legal currency status.

## Contribution Incentives

Contributors should be able to receive recognition for durable design influence.

Possible future mechanisms:

- credited motif lineage;
- contribution reputation;
- named design branches;
- prototype participation;
- royalties or prize pools when contractually established;
- physical editions;
- canon citations;
- institutional commissions.

No compensation should be implied until terms, ownership, licensing, and payment rules are explicit.

## Intellectual Property Boundary

Before contributions are used in commercial physical products, the system must establish:

- contributor identity;
- submission license;
- ownership status;
- permitted transformations;
- attribution terms;
- commercial-use rights;
- compensation terms when applicable;
- dispute and takedown procedures.

The platform must not silently absorb user ideas into commercial products.

## Product Translation

The future Tesseract Studio is the synthesis mechanism.

```txt
SLOP TROUGH captures abundance.

Tesseract Studio converts signal into engineered object candidates.
```
