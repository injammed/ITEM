# Caechat → AETIMM Selection Engine Fold Sequence

## Objective

Build a functioning, continually evolving product that can defensibly surface:

- the strongest 10% of the eligible AETIMM corpus;
- the strongest 1% of the eligible AETIMM corpus;
- a smaller canon-candidate set for human and expert review.

The system must not claim to identify the top 1% of all AI artifacts in existence until its observed corpus, external benchmarks, and coverage justify that claim.

```txt
SLOP TROUGH
→ ELIGIBILITY
→ SIGNAL
→ COMPARISON
→ REFINEMENT
→ TOP DECILE
→ TOP PERCENTILE
→ CANON CANDIDATES
→ MUSEUM
```

## Caechat Lineage

The inherited pattern is:

```txt
monitor
→ ingest
→ analyze / cluster / classify
→ AAA judgment gate
→ edit / refine
→ publish
→ verify
```

AAA does not output one magical universal score. It routes artifacts into:

```txt
CANON
CANDIDATE
REFINEMENT
ARCHIVE
REJECT
```

AETIMM preserves that logic while adding public entertainment, longitudinal judgment data, provenance, lineage, anti-gaming, and cross-media comparison.

---

## Hard Distinctions

### Popularity is not quality

High participation may place an artifact in Foam, Greatest Failure, or Most Divisive without making it Museum material.

### Top-rated is cohort-relative

Every percentile must name its comparison cohort:

- media type;
- publication window;
- minimum judgment count;
- provenance tier;
- language or modality when relevant;
- eligible safety and rights state.

### Canon is not percentile rank

The top 1% is a discovery and review pool. Canon requires provenance, rights, lineage, technical truth labeling, curator review, and corpus usefulness.

# Fold Sequence

## Fold S-01 — Artifact Envelope

Normalize every artifact into one lifecycle envelope while keeping modality-specific fields.

Required fields:

- artifact ID;
- creator ID;
- media type;
- hashes;
- provenance state;
- safety state;
- rights state;
- publication state;
- revision lineage;
- created and published times.

Success: every supported artifact can enter the same lifecycle without pretending image, code, music, proof, and simulation share one aesthetic scale.

## Fold S-02 — Eligibility Gate

Create a deterministic eligibility function before ranking.

An artifact is ranking-eligible only when:

- approved for public display;
- safety review passed;
- rights attestation exists;
- required metadata is present;
- not removed or under active dispute;
- not an exact duplicate;
- has enough exposure or judgments for the board being calculated.

Success: rankings never silently compare public artifacts against private, unsafe, disputed, or insufficiently observed artifacts.

## Fold S-03 — Public Signal Ledger

Store separate signals rather than one score:

```txt
preserve
refine
slop
novelty
coherence
entertainment
provenance
feasibility
lineage value
```

Add exposure counts so ten votes after ten impressions are not treated like ten votes after ten thousand impressions.

Success: every displayed aggregate can be reproduced from append-only evidence.

## Fold S-04 — Vote Confidence and Wilson Bounds

Replace naive percentages with uncertainty-aware estimates.

For each judgment dimension, calculate:

- sample size;
- observed rate;
- lower confidence bound;
- exposure-adjusted confidence;
- recency and cohort context.

Success: an artifact with 2/2 Preserve cannot outrank an artifact with 8,000/10,000 Preserve solely because 100% is numerically larger than 80%.

## Fold S-05 — Foam Boards

Launch entertainment boards that use participation without claiming canon:

- Foam Now;
- Fastest Rising;
- Greatest Failure;
- Most Divisive;
- Most Refined;
- Most Remixed.

Core law:

```txt
slop votes increase cultural signal
slop votes do not suppress visibility
```

Success: negative judgment cannot be used as griefing to bury an artifact from the entertainment layer.

## Fold S-06 — Similarity and Duplicate Graph

Detect exact duplicates, trivial variations, template clusters, remixes, and likely derivations.

Use the graph to:

- prevent upload flooding;
- reduce repeated-content dominance;
- attribute remixes;
- compare an artifact against its nearest peers;
- measure novelty relative to a relevant neighborhood.

Success: fifty nearly identical generations cannot occupy fifty top-percentile positions.

## Fold S-07 — Cohort Construction

Define fair comparison cohorts by:

- media type;
- artifact family;
- time window;
- provenance tier;
- maturity state;
- exposure band;
- language or domain where necessary.

Success: percentile claims always identify the denominator and cannot silently change populations.

## Fold S-08 — Multi-Dimensional Judgment

Produce interpretable component estimates rather than one public universal score:

- novelty;
- coherence;
- entertainment impact;
- provenance strength;
- technical feasibility;
- reproducibility;
- symbolic durability;
- refinement responsiveness;
- lineage value.

Success: users and curators can see why an artifact is strong and where it fails.

## Fold S-09 — Anti-Gaming Weighting

Reduce suspicious influence from:

- account farms;
- coordinated rings;
- creator self-amplification;
- burst brigading;
- duplicate accounts;
- repeated identical voting patterns;
- low-effort exposure manipulation.

Do not publicly accuse accounts automatically. Reduce influence, preserve evidence, and escalate uncertain cases for review.

Success: simulated collusion cannot reliably manufacture top-decile placement.

## Fold S-10 — Top-Decile Selector

Create the first serious selection pool.

An artifact enters the top 10% only within an explicit eligible cohort and only after:

- minimum exposure;
- minimum independent judgments;
- confidence threshold;
- duplicate/derivative adjustment;
- anti-gaming adjustment;
- no unresolved safety, rights, or provenance block.

The selector should return multiple ranked views rather than one absolute list:

- strongest entertainment artifact;
- strongest preservation candidate;
- strongest useful failure;
- strongest refinement response;
- strongest lineage contribution.

Success: rerunning the selector from the same evidence reproduces the same pool.

## Fold S-11 — Refinement Loop

Use Caechat's edit-and-rejudge lineage:

```txt
candidate
→ critique
→ revision
→ blind or separated re-evaluation
→ improvement measurement
```

Measure whether an artifact responds to critique without losing its valuable traits.

Success: the system can distinguish lucky first-pass spectacle from work that survives deliberate refinement.

## Fold S-12 — Trait Harvester and Lineage

Extract reusable traits from both successful artifacts and useful failures.

Every trait retains:

- source artifact;
- contributor;
- evidence;
- license/permission state;
- revision history;
- accepted grafts;
- downstream influence.

Success: an artifact can contribute to the Orchard even when the complete artifact never reaches the Museum.

## Fold S-13 — Top-Percentile Selector

The top 1% pool is selected from the top decile, not directly from raw uploads.

Required evidence should include:

- stable multi-dimensional strength;
- sufficient independent judgment;
- strong or improving provenance;
- derivative-context awareness;
- refinement evidence;
- anti-gaming clearance;
- curator review;
- lineage value or exceptional standalone value.

Success: the top-percentile pool is small enough for serious human review and large enough to preserve category diversity.

## Fold S-14 — Expert and Adversarial Review

Route technical claims to domain review:

- code executes and is maintainable;
- mathematical claims are checked;
- simulations expose assumptions;
- physical designs distinguish concept, prototype, and production readiness;
- factual claims are sourced;
- aesthetic artifacts receive criticism without pretending taste is objective truth.

Success: a technically false but popular artifact cannot enter canon under an undifferentiated popularity score.

## Fold S-15 — Canon Candidate Selector

AAA returns one of:

```txt
CANDIDATE
REFINEMENT
ARCHIVE
REJECT
```

Only reviewed candidates approach Canon. The selector nominates; it never canonizes autonomously.

Minimum conditions:

- provenance threshold met;
- rights resolved;
- meaningful judgment history;
- curator review complete;
- trait and derivation lineage visible;
- technical claims labeled and reviewed;
- no unresolved safety or fraud flags;
- clear corpus role.

## Fold S-16 — Museum Admission

Museum admission records:

- why the artifact was selected;
- cohort and percentile at selection time;
- component evidence;
- curator decision;
- lineage;
- known limitations;
- later reassessment history.

The Museum may evolve its presentation indefinitely while admission evidence remains stable and auditable.

## Fold S-17 — External Benchmark Corpus

To move beyond “top 1% of AETIMM,” ingest or license representative benchmark sets from the wider AI-artifact world.

Measure:

- retrieval recall of externally recognized works;
- disagreement with expert panels;
- category coverage;
- false-positive and false-negative rates;
- robustness across time and model generations.

Success: broader quality claims are based on measured coverage rather than branding.

## Fold S-18 — Longitudinal Outcome Calibration

Track whether selected artifacts later produce:

- sustained attention;
- meaningful revisions;
- influential traits;
- remixes;
- research or engineering utility;
- commercial products;
- historical relevance;
- durable Museum interest.

Use these outcomes to recalibrate models without rewriting past evidence.

Success: the selection engine improves from consequences, not merely immediate votes.

# Functional Product Milestones

## Selection MVP

A functioning first product exists when AETIMM can:

1. ingest approved artifacts;
2. collect separate judgment signals;
3. count exposure;
4. form explicit cohorts;
5. produce Foam boards;
6. identify a reproducible top decile;
7. show why each artifact qualified;
8. route candidates into refinement or Museum review.

## Serious Selection Product

The product becomes substantively defensible when it also has:

- duplicate/derivation graphs;
- anti-gaming weighting;
- refinement measurement;
- trait lineage;
- top-percentile selection;
- expert review paths;
- external benchmark evaluation;
- longitudinal outcome calibration.

---

# Initial Selection Formula Shape

Do not publish production weights. The architecture should resemble:

```txt
eligibility gate
× confidence-adjusted human signal
× provenance confidence
× novelty within cohort
× refinement response
× lineage value
× category-diversity adjustment
× anti-gaming trust
```

This is a candidate-selection function, not a truth function.

No artifact reaches the Museum merely because one scalar number is high.

---

# Core Moat

The defensible asset is not one formula.

It is the longitudinal dataset:

```txt
artifact
→ exposure
→ judgments
→ controversy
→ critique
→ revision
→ traits
→ lineage
→ commercial or canonical outcome
```

That is where Caechat becomes AETIMM: judgment is no longer an isolated answer. It becomes a recorded, revisable, consequence-tested selection system.
